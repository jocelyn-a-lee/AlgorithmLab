Welcome to Algorithm Lab

Algorithm Lab is an interactive exploration of mathematics and computer science through programming, visualization, and experimentation. Discover how algorithms work, why they matter, and how mathematical concepts become computational solutions.

Modules

Number Theory

Explore the mathematical foundations of computing through prime numbers, divisibility, modular arithmetic, and the Euclidean algorithm. Investigate how numbers interact and how algorithms solve mathematical problems efficiently.

Explore Number Theory

Graph Theory

Discover how algorithms navigate networks, find connections, and determine efficient paths. Explore graph structures, search algorithms, and the mathematics behind real-world networks.

Cryptography

Investigate the mathematics behind encryption and secure communication. Experiment with classical ciphers, modular arithmetic, and the computational principles that help protect digital information.

Purpose

Algorithm Lab was created to bridge the gap between mathematical theory and practical programming. By implementing algorithms, visualizing their behavior, and conducting computational experiments, this project aims to make complex concepts more understandable and accessible. The laboratory also serves as an ongoing independent research and development project, documenting the process of exploring mathematics, computer science, and their applications in the real world.
